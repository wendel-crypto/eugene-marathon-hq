Eugene Marathon Training HQ

A training app I built for my own race: the 2027 Eugene Marathon. It holds a 22-week plan, pushes every workout to my Garmin watch, reads my recovery numbers each morning and adjusts the day before I lace up.

The problem

A plan on paper stops matching the runner within a week.

Training plans are static. Sleep, heat, humidity and work are not. I was copying workouts into my watch by hand, checking four Garmin screens every morning and guessing whether to push or back off.

I wanted one place that knows the plan and my body's numbers, tells me what to run today, and puts the session on my wrist without typing it in.

The product

Four screens, built for a phone at 5am.

How it works

The app is the surface. Agents do the work behind it.

01

Plan as data

The 22 weeks are generated from a script: phases, paces by phase, track sessions on Tuesday and Friday, holiday swaps. Each step is split into 1 km blocks so the watch laps cleanly.

02

Garmin sync

Workouts upload to Garmin Connect with pace or heart-rate targets, so the watch alerts when I drift off target mid-run.

03

Morning check

A scheduled task reads training readiness, sleep, HRV, resting heart rate and the forecast at 4:45am and marks the day green, amber or red, with the adjusted session.

04

Coach feedback

After each synced run a second task compares splits and heart rate with the target and writes a short review. On Sunday a third reviews the week and re-syncs the next one.

05

Fitness gate

Paces only get faster when the watch's threshold pace and race prediction support it, with a tune-up half marathon in week 12 as the checkpoint for the final target.

06

Share cards

A small standalone page draws the run card on a canvas and hands it to the phone's share sheet, because the app's sandbox cannot reach Photos.

My role

Product, UX and direction. Claude wrote the code.

I defined the product, the screens and the rules a coach would apply, then iterated on a phone until it was fast to read. The build was done in conversation with Claude, from the plan generator to the Garmin integration and the scheduled agents.

The plan starts on 23 November 2026, so race results are still to come. This page will be updated after Eugene.
