Heart-rate zones

Your Garmin running zones (max 198). Each day card names its target zone. The morning check reads your time in each zone from yesterday's run.

Z2119–138Easy runs and most of every long run. 80% of your week belongs here.

Z5178+VO2 reps and the last km of a race
  • If more than a quarter of an easy run was in Z3 or higher, the next morning counts one amber flag and your next easy run is capped at 135 bpm.
  • If threshold reps drift into Z5, the Sunday review slows threshold pace by 3 s/km. If they sit low in Z4 two weeks running, paces get faster.
  • Short reps (200–400 m) stay on pace targets, because heart rate lags too far behind to steer them.

Totals: 4 Gel 160, 3 Gel 100 CAF 100, 1 Gel 100. About 235 g of carbs on course (roughly 75 g an hour) and 300 mg of caffeine. Water at every aid station. Carry 1 LMNT in a soft flask for the first half.

Training

  • Easy runs under 60 min: no gels. Half an LMNT in 500 ml before or after.
  • Track: 1 Gel 100 and 1 LMNT in 500 ml, 20–30 min before. Nothing during.
  • Long runs up to 16 km: 1 Gel 100 at 45 min.
  • 18–22 km: Gel 100 every 35 min.
  • 24–30 km: Gel 100 every 30 min, the third one CAF 100.
  • 32 km and up, or any marathon-pace block: race rehearsal. Gel 160 every 25 min, the third and sixth CAF 100.
  • LMNT on long runs: 1 stick before, then 1 per 75 min of running in 500–750 ml. Add one on sauna days.
  • Caffeine: 300 mg on race day is about 4.5 mg per kg for you. Count your coffee too and stay near 400 mg for the day. Practise it on long runs first.

The step up to 75 g an hour needs training too. If you have been told to limit salt or caffeine, check this with your doctor.

Where to run

  • Tropical Park track (Tue, Fri 5:30am). Flat, elevation counted as 0 m. Use the Track Run profile so laps snap to 400 m. Pace targets are trusted here.
  • Key Biscayne (long runs). The only real climb is the Powell Bridge on the Rickenbacker, about 20–25 m per crossing. Reviews count that and ignore the rest of the GPS elevation.
  • Brickell (easy and recovery). Towers bounce the GPS, so pace and elevation jump around. Easy runs here are heart-rate only and reviews treat Brickell as flat.

How it adjusts itself

  • GREEN Readiness 60+, HRV in range, Body Battery 60+. Run as planned.
  • AMBER Readiness 40–59, HRV 10% under your 7-day average, Body Battery 35–59, under 6 h sleep, or weight down 1.5% overnight. Reps cut by a quarter, easy runs by 20%.
  • RED Readiness under 40, two amber flags, or resting heart rate up 7 bpm. Rest or 30 min very easy. The session moves to the next free day.
  • Heat. Temperature °F plus dew point °F at run time: up to 120 no change, then pace targets slow 1% to 5%. Above 160, treadmill.
  • Holidays. No sessions on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day or New Year's Day. Friday track moves to Thursday those weeks.
  • Watch alerts. Every work step has a pace or heart-rate range, so the watch buzzes and shows too fast, too slow, high or low as soon as you leave it. Recovery jogs have no range on purpose.
  • Sunday 2:17pm review. Grades the week after your long run, reshapes the next weeks, and syncs 14 days to Garmin.
