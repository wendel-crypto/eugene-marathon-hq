Totals: 4 Gel 160 and 3 Gel 100 CAF 100. About 210 g of carbs on course (roughly 70 g an hour) and 300 mg of caffeine. Water at every aid station.

Training

  • Easy runs under 60 min: no gels. Half an LMNT in 500 ml.
  • Track: 1 Gel 100 and 1 LMNT in 500 ml, 20–30 min before. Nothing during.
  • Long runs up to 16 km: 1 Gel 100 at 45 min. 18–22 km: Gel 100 every 35 min.
  • 24–30 km: Gel 100 every 30 min, the third one CAF 100.
  • 32 km and up, or any marathon-pace block: race rehearsal. Gel 160 every 25 min, the third and sixth CAF 100.
  • LMNT on long runs: 1 stick before, then 1 per 75 min of running. Add one on sauna days.
  • Caffeine: 300 mg on race day is about 4.5 mg per kg for you. Count coffee too and stay near 400 mg for the day.

If you have been told to limit salt or caffeine, check this with your doctor.

Where to run
  • Tropical Park track (Tue, Fri 5:30am). Flat, counted as 0 m of climb. Use the Track Run profile so laps snap to 400 m.
  • Key Biscayne (long runs). The only real climb is the Powell Bridge, about 20–25 m per crossing.
  • Brickell (easy and recovery). Towers bounce the GPS, so these runs are judged by heart rate, never pace.

How the plan adjusts itself
  • GREEN Readiness 60+, HRV in range, Body Battery 60+. Run as planned.
  • AMBER Readiness 40–59, HRV 10% under your 7-day average, Body Battery 35–59, under 6 h sleep, weight down 1.5% overnight, or yesterday's easy run ran hot. Reps cut by a quarter, easy runs by 20%.
  • RED Readiness under 40, two amber flags, or resting heart rate up 7 bpm. Rest or 30 min very easy.
  • Heat. Temperature °F plus dew point °F at run time: up to 120 no change, then pace targets slow 1% to 5%. Above 160, treadmill.
  • Holidays. No sessions on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day or New Year's Day.
  • Session feedback. Written after each run syncs, checked at 8:41am, 1:41pm and 8:41pm.
  • Sunday 2:17pm review. Grades the week, writes the summary, reshapes the next weeks and syncs 14 days to Garmin.
  • Watch alerts. Every work step has a pace or heart-rate range, so the watch buzzes when you leave it.
